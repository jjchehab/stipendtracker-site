How to use it

From zero to tracking in two minutes

Follow along with these short clips. Every step uses the real app, with made-up numbers.

1

Set up your budget in five steps

The first time you open StipendTracker, a short setup walks you through five numbered steps, like checking out in an online shop. Choose your language and currency, pick the day your pay period starts, enter any savings you already have as your starting Baller Budget, set a budget for each category, then review everything and start. Use Back and Next to move between steps. Nothing you typed is lost.

  • Paid on the 25th? Pick Day 25 and each period runs from the 25th to the 24th. You can also choose the last day of the month.
  • Turn on Fixed bill for things you pay in one go, like rent.
  • Everything can be changed later in Settings.

2

Add what you spend

Pick a category, type the amount and press Enter. A name is optional but handy later. Bought something yesterday? Change the date. Made a mistake? Undo last entry takes it back.

  • Paid in another currency? Tap €, $ or £ next to the amount and it converts at that day's exchange rate.
  • Rent or a subscription every month? Add it once under Recurring entries in Settings and it is added for you on its day. Monthly only.
  • You get a gentle heads-up if an entry puts a category behind pace.
  • The app comes in English and Spanish. Switch the language in Settings.

3

Read the pace bars

Each bar shows what you have spent, and the white mark shows where you should be by today. Green means on pace, yellow a little high, red way over. For the first three days of a month only going over a full budget is flagged, so early bills do not set off false alarms.

  • Fixed bills like rent are measured against their full amount.
  • Budget changes apply from this month on. Past months keep theirs.

4

Use your Baller Budget

Whatever you do not spend in a finished month rolls into your Baller Budget. Add your own funds too, like a gift or a bonus, with Manage. When you want to spend freely, pick Baller Budget as the category.

  • If it ever drops below zero, the card turns red.
  • Every deposit and spend is listed, and you can delete any of them.
5

Look back at any month

Budget History lists every month. Click one to see a ring chart of where the money went, with over-budget categories flagged yellow or red. Each month remembers the budgets it had, so changing budgets later never rewrites the past.

  • Hover a category to highlight its slice.
  • Spending by Category in the menu lists every entry in a category.

6

See where you are heading

Trends turns your entries into a few clear pictures. See this month against your usual, a projected total for the month, a pace line, a safe amount to spend per day, and a calendar that shades each day from green to red. Streaks and a budget check-up are here too.

  • Hover over or tap a bar to see the split by category. The dashed box on the current month shows where you are projected to finish.
  • Finish periods under budget to build a streak. The budget check-up suggests which budgets could be lowered and which are running high.
  • Early in a period the projection uses your average. After the halfway point it switches to your pace.
  • Some boxes need history: comparisons need two finished periods and the check-up needs three.

7

Import your bank statement

You do not need to enter every little transaction one at a time. Download your account movements from your bank, as a PDF from Santander or as a CSV file from most banks, such as Chase. Choose Import bank file and StipendTracker finds the purchases for you. If it does not recognise a CSV, a quick screen lets you match the columns. Review the purchases, change any category, and tick Remember so that store is sorted automatically next time. Then click Import.

  • Importing dollars into a euro budget? Pick the currency of the file and every purchase is converted at the exchange rate from its own date.
  • Purchases you already imported are skipped, even when two files overlap.
  • Incoming payments are skipped. Only spending is imported.
  • Imported by mistake? Undo import removes the whole batch.
  • The file is read on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere.

8

Keep a backup

Your data lives only on your device. In Settings, Save backup file downloads a copy you can keep somewhere safe. Restore from backup brings it back, even on another device.

  • Saving one after each month takes ten seconds. The app reminds you once a month.
  • Want your numbers in a spreadsheet? Export CSV downloads your entries for Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.
  • Each device keeps its own data. Nothing syncs between them.
Tip: install the web app on your phone or computer and it opens like a normal app, even with no internet. See the install guide.
