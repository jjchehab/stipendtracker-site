FAQ

Questions, answered

Quick answers about privacy, data, trends and the bank import.

Basics

Is StipendTracker free?

Yes. There is nothing to buy and no subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Open it and start.

Which devices does it work on?

The web app works on Windows, Mac, iPhone, Android and Chromebook, in any modern browser, and you can install it like an app. See the Download page for the steps.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After you open the web app once, it keeps a copy on your device and opens with no internet. Installing it to your home screen or Start menu makes this even smoother.

Is it in Spanish?

Yes. Both this website and the app are available in English and Spanish. The app starts in English, and you can switch to Spanish at any time.

How do I change the app's language?

Open Settings and choose English or Español under Language. In the first step of the setup, you can also pick the language. The app remembers your choice on that device. Your data and category names are never changed when you switch. If you set up the app in Spanish, the starting categories have Spanish names.

Can I use dollars instead of euros?

Yes. Choose €, $ or £ as your budget currency in setup or in Settings. You can also log a single purchase in either of the other currencies, and the app converts it for you.

Privacy and your data

Where is my data stored?

Only on your own device. The app uses your browser's storage on that device. There are no accounts and no servers, so nobody else can see your numbers.

Does the app connect to the internet?

Only for one thing: exchange rates. If you log an entry or import a file in another currency, the app asks a free public service for the rate, sending just the two currencies and the date (for example, dollars to euros on one day). Your amounts, entry names, categories and budgets are never sent. The rate is saved so the app keeps working offline.

Will my data sync between my devices?

No. Each device keeps its own data and they do not share it. That is the trade-off for keeping everything private. Pick one main device, and use backup files if you need to move data.

What if I lose my phone or clear my browser data?

The data goes with it, unless you saved a backup. In Settings, use Save backup file now and then (after each month is a good habit) and keep the file somewhere safe. Restore from backup brings everything back.

Why does the iPhone page say to install it?

Safari can clear a regular website's data after about a week without use. A web app added to the Home Screen is exempt from that, so installing protects your data.

Is it safe to import my bank file?

The file is read right on your device. It is never uploaded, and the app does not connect to your bank. You choose what to import, and the file itself is not kept.

Using the app

What is the Baller Budget?

A savings buffer. Whatever you do not spend in a finished month rolls into it, and you can add your own funds too. When you want to spend without guilt, pick Baller Budget as the category.

What does the pace bar mean?

The coloured bar is what you have spent. The white mark is where you should be by today if you spent evenly through the month. Green is on pace, yellow is a little high and red is way over.

Why do I not get warnings in the first days of the month?

Early in the month, big bills can make a category look off track when it is not. For the first three days, only going over a full budget is flagged. Pace warnings start on day four.

What is a fixed bill?

Something you pay in one go, like rent. Fixed bills are measured against their full amount instead of a day-by-day pace, so they do not look overspent the day they are paid.

What does the Trends page show?

A set of boxes: this month against your usual, spending per period with a projected total, your pace through the month, how much is safe to spend per day, a calendar of daily spending, your Baller Budget over time and your biggest purchases. Streaks and the budget check-up are in Trends too.

How is the projected total worked out?

In the first half of a period it uses the average of your finished periods, because a few early purchases say little about the whole period. After the halfway point it switches to your pace: what you have spent, plus your daily average on everyday categories for the days that remain, plus any fixed bills not yet paid.

What are streaks and the budget check-up?

A streak counts how many finished periods in a row you stayed under your total budget, and category streaks do the same for single categories. The budget check-up looks at your last few finished periods and suggests which budgets could be lowered, which are running high and which look right-sized. It needs three finished periods.

What does Safe to spend per day mean?

What is left of this period's budget, minus any fixed bills you have not paid yet, divided by the days remaining. It turns red when you are over budget.

Can I add recurring entries?

Yes, monthly ones. In Settings, add something like rent or a subscription once, choose the day of the month, and the app adds it for you every month. You can remove it at any time.

Can I change my budgets later?

Yes, in Settings. Changes apply from the current month onward. Past months keep the budgets they had, so your history stays honest.

Can I log a purchase in another currency?

Yes. In the new-entry form, tap the € or $ button next to the amount. The app converts it into the currency of your budget using that day's exchange rate, and shows both amounts in the entry log. The rate is locked in when you add the entry, so your history never shifts later. If you are offline, it uses the last rate it saved.

How do I fix a wrong entry?

Use Undo last entry right after adding it. For an older one, open its category in the side menu and press Edit next to the entry. You can change the amount, category, date and name. Delete removes it.

What is a pay period, and can I start it on pay day?

Yes. During setup (or later in Settings) pick the day your stipend arrives, from 1 to 28 or the last day of the month. Each period runs from that day to the day before the next one, and your history, budgets and Baller Budget follow the same periods. If you change the day later, it applies from your next period, so past ones are never rewritten.

Can I export my entries to a spreadsheet?

Yes. In Settings, choose This period or Everything under Export to a spreadsheet. You get a CSV file that opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets. Any past period also has an Export CSV button.

Will the app remind me to back up?

Yes, gently. If more than a month has passed since your last backup, a banner on the home screen offers to save one with a single tap, or to remind you later.

How do I start over?

In Settings, choose Reset all data. It asks you to confirm, then takes you back to setup. Save a backup first if you might want the old data.

Bank import

Which banks does the import support?

Santander (Spain) account-movements PDFs, and CSV files from most banks. It recognises the layouts of several common banks, such as Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Citi, Capital One and BBVA. For any other bank, a quick screen lets you match the columns yourself. If a file does not work, email me and I will look into it.

My bank only gives me an Excel file. What can I do?

Open it in Excel or Numbers, choose File, then Save As (or Export), then CSV. Then import that CSV file.

Can I import a file in another currency?

Yes. On the review screen, choose the currency of the file. Each purchase is converted into your budget currency using the exchange rate from its own date, and the entry keeps the original amount and the rate used. It needs an internet connection once to look up the rates. Rates you have used before are saved on your device.

What if the app does not recognise my CSV?

A Match the columns screen opens with its best guesses filled in. Choose which column is the date, the description and the amount (or money out and money in), say whether purchases show as negative or positive, and check the preview. You can reopen it any time with Adjust columns.

Does it connect to my bank account?

No. You download a file from your bank and add it yourself. That is why nothing leaves your device.

What does the Remember checkbox do?

It remembers which category you picked for a store, so next time that store is sorted automatically. Leave it unticked for stores where you buy different kinds of things.

Will it import the same purchase twice?

No. Purchases you have already imported are recognised and skipped, even when two files overlap, such as a week and then the whole month. If an import goes wrong, Undo import removes that whole batch.
