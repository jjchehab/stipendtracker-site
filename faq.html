FAQ

Questions, answered

Quick answers about privacy, data, features and the bank import.

Basics

Is StipendTracker free?

Yes. There is nothing to buy and no subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Open it and start.

Which devices does it work on?

The web app works on Windows, Mac, iPhone, Android and Chromebook, in any modern browser. There is also a Mac app. See the Download page.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After you open the web app once, it keeps a copy on your device and opens with no internet. Installing it to your home screen or Start menu makes this even smoother.

Is it in Spanish?

This website is available in English and Spanish. The app itself is in English for now.

Can I use dollars instead of euros?

Yes. Choose € or $ in setup or in Settings. Only the symbol changes. Nothing is converted.

Privacy and your data

Where is my data stored?

Only on your own device. The web app uses your browser's storage, and the Mac app saves a file on your Mac. There are no accounts and no servers, so nobody else can see your numbers.

Does the app connect to the internet?

Only for one thing: exchange rates. If you log an entry in the other currency, the app asks a free public service for the rate, sending just the two currencies and the date (for example, dollars to euros on one day). Your amounts, entry names, categories and budgets are never sent. The rate is saved so the app keeps working offline.

Will my data sync between my devices?

No. Each device keeps its own data, and the web and Mac apps do not share. That is the trade-off for keeping everything private. Pick one main device, and use backup files if you need to move data.

What if I lose my phone or clear my browser data?

The data goes with it, unless you saved a backup. In Settings, use Save backup file now and then (after each month is a good habit) and keep the file somewhere safe. Restore from backup brings everything back.

Why does the iPhone page say to install it?

Safari can clear a regular website's data after about a week without use. A web app added to the Home Screen is exempt from that, so installing protects your data.

Is it safe to import my bank PDF?

The PDF is read right on your device. It is never uploaded, and the app does not connect to your bank. You choose what to import, and the PDF itself is not kept.

Using the app

What is the Baller Budget?

A savings buffer. Whatever you do not spend in a finished month rolls into it, and you can add your own funds too. When you want to spend without guilt, pick Baller Budget as the category.

What does the pace bar mean?

The coloured bar is what you have spent. The white mark is where you should be by today if you spent evenly through the month. Green is on pace, yellow is a little high and red is way over.

Why do I not get warnings in the first days of the month?

Early in the month, big bills can make a category look off track when it is not. For the first three days, only going over a full budget is flagged. Pace warnings start on day four.

What is a fixed bill?

Something you pay in one go, like rent. Fixed bills are measured against their full amount instead of a day-by-day pace, so they do not look overspent the day they are paid.

Can I change my budgets later?

Yes, in Settings. Changes apply from the current month onward. Past months keep the budgets they had, so your history stays honest.

Can I log a purchase in another currency?

Yes. In the new-entry form, tap the € or $ button next to the amount. The app converts it into the currency of your budget using that day's exchange rate, and shows both amounts in the entry log. The rate is locked in when you add the entry, so your history never shifts later. If you are offline, it uses the last rate it saved.

How do I fix a wrong entry?

Use Undo last entry right after adding it. For an older one, open the category in the side menu, delete it from the list and add it again.

How do I start over?

In Settings, choose Reset all data. It asks you to confirm, then takes you back to setup. Save a backup first if you might want the old data.

Bank import

Which banks does the import support?

Right now it reads the account-movements PDF from Santander (Spain). Other banks' PDFs may not work yet. If yours does not, email me and I will look into it.

Does it connect to my bank account?

No. You download a PDF from your bank and drop it in yourself. That is why nothing leaves your device.

What does the Remember checkbox do?

It remembers which category you picked for a store, so next time that store is sorted automatically. Leave it unticked for stores where you buy different kinds of things.

Will it import the same purchase twice?

No. Purchases you have already imported are recognised and skipped. If an import goes wrong, Undo import removes that whole batch.
