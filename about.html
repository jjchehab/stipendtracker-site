About us

Built by someone living the stipend life

I am JJ Chehab. I am living overseas on a federal grant, and like many stipend and fellowship recipients I get one fixed amount to cover everything for the month: rent, groceries, travel and the occasional dinner out.

Adjusting to life in a new country makes money harder to read. Prices are unfamiliar, bills arrive in unfamiliar ways, and you are often thinking in two currencies at once. I wanted a way to navigate my spending that did not need a finance degree or a sprawling spreadsheet.

So I built StipendTracker. It began as a Google Sheet, became a Mac app, and is now a website you can install on almost any device. We made it so that anyone adjusting to a new place can see, at a glance, whether they are on track, and relax a little about the rest.

The thinking behind it

Why it works the way it does

Every feature started as a small frustration I wanted to get rid of.

Pace, not just totals

A budget total tells you what is left, but not whether you are spending too fast. Showing where you should be by today catches problems while there is still time to fix them.

A grace period at the start

Rent and other big bills land early in the month. For the first three days, only going over a full budget is flagged, so a normal start never looks like an emergency.

The Baller Budget

Spending less than planned should feel like a win. The surplus rolls into a buffer you can spend without guilt, and it turns red if you dip below zero.

Bank import

Typing in every coffee gets old fast. Importing a statement turns a long evening of entry into a minute of checking.

Privacy by design

There are no accounts and no servers. Your data stays on your device, so there is nothing to sign up for and nothing for anyone else to see.

The logo

A dollar sign that turns into a euro, split by a slash. It is a small nod to budgeting between two worlds.

Why this matters

A stipend is a fixed income at a moment of big change, often far from home. A few weeks of unplanned overspending can turn into real stress when you are new somewhere. Clear, honest tracking turns worry into a plan. If StipendTracker helps one person feel calm about their month, it was worth building.

Give it a try, and tell me what you think.
